Why Executive Mind Literacy Matters

Every decision passes through a way of thinking.

Leaders regularly make decisions under pressure, interpret incomplete information, manage relationships, and respond to changing circumstances. Yet the thinking processes behind those decisions are not always examined.

The aim is not to eliminate bias, control every emotion, or make perfect decisions. It is to notice more — and to choose more deliberately.

Executive Mind Literacy introduces a practical way to reflect on:

  1. How assumptions influence interpretation.
  2. How previous experiences shape responses.
  3. How emotions can influence decisions.
  4. How established thinking habits affect judgement.
  5. How conscious reflection can create opportunities for different responses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions, answered.

What is Executive Mind Literacy?

A practical approach to understanding how assumptions, interpretations, emotional responses, and established thinking habits influence the way leaders interpret situations and make decisions.

Is this a conventional leadership training programme?

No. Rather than teaching leadership models or techniques, it focuses on the thinking behind your decisions, using structured reflection and practical exercises connected to a real leadership situation.

Who should participate?

Executives, senior managers, business owners, entrepreneurs, and professionals with leadership responsibilities who want to understand how their own thinking influences their judgement.

How long is the workshop?

Five days, with one two-hour session each day — ten hours of learning in total.

What is the total price?

US$50 per participant for the full five-day workshop, including the materials listed above.

Do I need previous knowledge of psychology?

No. The workshop is designed for business leaders and uses plain, practical language. No background in psychology is required.

Will I receive practical materials?

Yes. You receive a Personal Reflection Guide, four weekly self-reflection worksheets for independent practice, and the personalised 30-day Leadership Improvement Plan you develop during the workshop.

Is this a psychological assessment?

No. Neither the free Leadership Blind-Spot Profile nor the workshop is a psychological assessment, diagnosis, or therapy. Both are reflection tools intended to support self-awareness.

How do I reserve my place?

Complete the registration form on this page. Each workshop is limited to 12 participants.
